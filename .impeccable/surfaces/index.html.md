# Oslo Adventure planner

## Scope and mode

The single-page web planner for a friend visiting Oslo. Visitor mode: Operate, with an editorial/experience layer.

## Audience and job

Friends and first-time visitors need to choose a few good places, see how they relate geographically, and leave with a simple day they are excited to follow.

## Task and proof

Filter the curated edit by coffee, places, or sauna; select a place from the list or schematic map; inspect the local rationale; and add up to three stops to the morning/afternoon/evening day strip. Real supplied imagery and source links make the edit feel grounded.

## Constraints

The first release is static, dependency-free, and deployable on GitHub Pages. It does not claim live opening hours, pricing, bookings, or turn-by-turn routing. It must remain usable on a phone in daylight and support keyboard focus, reduced motion, and screen-reader labels.

## Direction and memorable moment

The page is a bright fjord field note: panorama first, then library beside map, then a route-shaped day strip. The memorable moment is choosing a pin and watching the same place resolve across list, map, detail, and day plan.
